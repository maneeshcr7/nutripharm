Sourcing FAQs

Before you request a quote

What makes this Cordyceps different from standard extracts?

It’s standardized to a specific Cordycepin range (1–8%) and processed with our proprietary Bio-Enh Technology, which is what makes it fully water-soluble and gives it a multi-fold increase in bioavailability versus conventional extracts.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For powders and extracts, the minimum order is 10 kg. For capsule, tablet and packaged formats, the minimum order is 500 units. Both are set on the enquiry form when you request a quote.

Can Cordycepin and Adenosine percentages be customized?

Yes. Cordycepin can be standardized between 1–8% and Adenosine between 0.2–2%, depending on your finished-product requirements and industry — nutraceutical, cosmeceutical or aquaculture.

What certifications does this Cordyceps carry?

Our facility and process are FSSAI, ISO and GMP certified, and Cordyceps itself carries GRAS status with the FDA and is listed in the pharmacopoeias of China, Japan and Korea.

Do you supply cosmeceutical and aquaculture use?

All three. The standardized extract (Cordy Maxx) in particular is customized specifically for nutraceutical, cosmeceutical and aquaculture inputs.
