Why double extraction

One phase never gets the whole mushroom.

Mushroom bioactives split across two solubility classes: the polysaccharides come out in water, the triterpenes and sterols come out in ethanol. Extract in one phase only and half the profile stays in the biomass.

We run both — ultrasonication-assisted aqueous extraction alongside ethanol extraction — then purify, standardize and suspend the concentrate in a glycerol-based medium. The result is an alcohol-reduced liquid at 1:4 strength, built for high purity, consistent potency, enhanced stability and extended shelf life.

What this means for your product

A premium liquid format that drops straight into shots, beverages, oral sprays, drops and gummies — standardized per batch, with potency tailored to your formulation.
