A modern wellness marvel, an old medicine

Broad-spectrum by tradition. Standardized by us.

Cordyceps has been revered for over 500 years across Southeast Asia as a broad-spectrum therapeutic, and is officially listed in the pharmacopoeias of China, Japan and Korea for managing a wide range of medical ailments. Often considered one of the best preventive ingredients of our time, it carries GRAS status with the FDA.

We built India’s first fully water-soluble, standardized Cordyceps extract on top of that legacy — so formulators get a raw material that behaves the same way every time it’s weighed out.
