Vivid Nutripharm · Cordyceps page · Section study

Four ways to present the Cordyceps benefit set

Same twelve documented actions, same brand palette and type stack as the live site — four completely different structures. The current radial infographic reads as a single flat image; each of these is real HTML, so it reflows on mobile, indexes as text, and can carry interaction. Pick one and it goes into durt-cordyceps-range.html in place of the SVG.

01 · Monograph 02 · System map 03 · Mosaic 04 · Spec deck
01

The Monograph

A pharmacopoeia entry, not a poster. Zero icons, zero cards — the structure is carried entirely by numbering, system tags, hairline rules and the marker compound under each claim. Reads as documentation, which is what a B2B formulator buying on specification actually wants.

Best for
The B2B ingredient page, directly under the heritage copy
Density
All 12 actions visible at once, no scrolling past
Trade-off
Sober by design — no visual hook for consumer traffic
02

The System Map

Organises the claims by the body system they act on and puts them on a schematic figure. One benefit at a time, in depth, with its mechanism and marker read out beside it. The dark ground separates it from everything above and below on the page.

Best for
The anchor moment of the page — the thing people remember
Interaction
Click the rail or a hotspot; keyboard and screen-reader ready
Trade-off
Only one claim is visible at rest; most build effort of the four
03

The Mosaic

An asymmetric tile field where weight equals importance: the eight primary actions get full tiles, the four secondary ones collapse into a single list tile, and the product photo becomes a tile rather than a centrepiece. Closest in spirit to the current design while behaving properly on a phone.

Best for
The safest swap — familiar rhythm, immediate scan
Extras
Absorbs the 500+ years and 1–8% Cordycepin figures as tiles
Trade-off
The most conventional of the four; needs the photo to sing
04

The Spec Deck

Twelve assay cards on a horizontal snap rail, dark and technical, each stamped with the marker compound the claim rests on. Vertical space stays tight — the whole benefit set costs one screen height instead of five — and the sideways motion signals "there is more here" without a scroll cue.

Best for
Long pages where the benefit set must not dominate
Interaction
Drag, arrows, or tick rail; snaps card to card
Trade-off
Horizontal scroll hides claims 4–12 until someone moves

All four run on the existing tokens — #1f8f3f, #0d5c2e, #eef4ea, Poppins / Inter / IBM Plex Mono — so whichever you pick sits inside the current page without a restyle. The rust accent (#c4652a) in options 02 and 04 is picked from the cordyceps itself and is the only new colour.

Copy is carried over verbatim from the current infographic. The system tags and marker compounds are additions — say the word if you'd rather they came out, or if any claim needs softening for regulatory review.
