FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers for brands evaluating manufacturing partners.

What kind of products can Vivid Nutripharm manufacture?

We manufacture across capsules, tablets, effervescents, liquid extracts & tinctures, oral sprays, and full-spectrum Cordyceps formats — spanning metabolic wellness, women’s health, respiratory health, sleep & stress, and performance categories.

Do you offer private label / white label manufacturing?

Yes — we support brands from concept and formulation through to packaging, regulatory support, and market-ready production.

What certifications does Vivid Nutripharm hold?

We’re DPIIT Recognized, FSSAI Certified, ISO 9001:2015 certified, GMP Certified, and compliant with EU Organic and USDA Organic standards.

What is your typical development timeline?

Timelines vary by product complexity and testing requirements — our team will give you a clear timeline once we understand your formulation and volume needs.

What is BioEnh™ and how is it different?

BioEnh™ is our proprietary bioavailability enhancement technology, designed to help the body absorb and use active ingredients more effectively than conventional formulations.
