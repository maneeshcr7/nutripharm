Our Technology Platforms

Advanced delivery systems engineered for better bioavailability, faster absorption, and formulation flexibility — the science behind every Vivid Nutripharm product.

See It In Our Products

BioEnh™ Technology

Re-engineers how actives are delivered, improving solubility and absorption so the body uses more of what’s in every dose.

Phytosome Technology

Wraps plant actives in a phospholipid shell that mirrors the body’s own cell membranes, for smoother, faster uptake of botanicals.

Oral Spray Platform

Fast-absorbing sublingual format that delivers precisely dosed actives straight into the bloodstream — no capsules or water needed.

Full-Spectrum Cordyceps

Every bioactive compound of the mushroom preserved across capsule, powder, and extract formats — the complete profile, not an isolated fraction.
